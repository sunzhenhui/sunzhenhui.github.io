VecLI: A framework for calculating vector landscape indices considering landscape fragmentation

Yao Yao, Tao Cheng, Zhenhui Sun, Linlong Li, Dongsheng Chen, Ziheng Chen, Jianglin Wei, Qingfeng Guan

Environmental Modelling & Software · 2022

DOI: 10.1016/j.envsoft.2022.105325

Paper DOI Code

Abstract

VecLI proposes a novel framework for calculating vector-based landscape indices that explicitly accounts for landscape fragmentation, enabling more accurate and fine-grained landscape pattern analysis in GIS environments.
