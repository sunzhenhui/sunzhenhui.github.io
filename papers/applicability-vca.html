Applicability and sensitivity analysis of vector cellular automata model for land cover change

Yao Yao, Ying Jiang, Zhenhui Sun, Linlong Li, Dongsheng Chen, Kailu Xiong, Anning Dong, Tao Cheng, Haoyan Zhang, Xun Liang et al.

Computers, Environment and Urban Systems · 2024

DOI: 10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2024.102090 · ISSN: 0198-9715

Paper DOI Code

Abstract

This research investigates the applicability and sensitivity of vector-based cellular automata models for land cover change simulation, evaluating model robustness and key parameter sensitivities across diverse geographic contexts.
