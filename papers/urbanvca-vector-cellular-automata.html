UrbanVCA: a vector-based cellular automata framework to simulate the urban land-use change at the land-parcel level

Yao Yao, Chenxi Liu, Zhenhui Sun, Kun Zhou, Dongsheng Chen, Linlong Li, Qingfeng Guan

arXiv preprint · 2021

arXiv: 2103.12444

Paper DOI Code

Abstract

UrbanVCA introduces a vector-based cellular automata framework designed to simulate urban land-use change at the land-parcel level, providing a more realistic and fine-grained alternative to traditional raster-based CA models.
