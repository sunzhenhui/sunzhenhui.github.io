Fine-grained regional economic forecasting for a megacity using vector-based cellular automata

Yao Yao, Haoyan Zhang, Zhenhui Sun, Linlong Li, Tao Cheng, Ying Jiang, Qingfeng Guan, Dongsheng Chen

International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation · December 2023

DOI: 10.1016/j.jag.2023.103602

Paper DOI Code

Abstract

This study proposes a vector-based cellular automata approach for fine-grained regional economic forecasting in megacities, integrating spatial heterogeneity and land parcel-level dynamics for improved prediction accuracy.
