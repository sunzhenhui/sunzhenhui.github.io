Uncovering spatiotemporal development patterns of AIDS in China: A study using panel data with Joinpoint Regression analysis and Spatial Clustering

Shu-nan Gui, Xiang Zhang, Zhenhui Sun, Yao Yao

Health & Place · 2024

DOI: 10.1016/j.healthplace.2024.103353 · ISSN: 1353-8292

Paper DOI Code

Abstract

This study investigates the spatiotemporal development patterns of AIDS in China using panel data, employing Joinpoint Regression analysis and Spatial Clustering methods to identify key trends and regional disparities.
