Estimating China's poverty reduction efficiency by integrating multi-source geospatial data and deep learning techniques

Yao Yao, Jianfeng Zhou, Zhenhui Sun, Qingfeng Guan, Zhiqiang Guo, Yin Xu, Jinbao Zhang, Ye Hong, Yuyang Cai, Ruoyu Wang

Geo-spatial Information Science · July 2024

DOI: 10.1080/10095020.2023.2165975

Paper DOI

Abstract

This study integrates multi-source geospatial data with deep learning techniques to estimate the efficiency of China's poverty reduction efforts, offering a data-driven framework for evaluating and guiding poverty alleviation policies.
