CoCA: Spatial cooperative simulation and future prediction of "land-population-economy" in urban agglomerations

Chenglong Zeng, Yao Yao, Jiayao Liu, Zhenhui Sun, Kun Zhou, Dongsheng Chen, Qingfeng Guan

Landscape and Urban Planning · November 2025

DOI: 10.1016/j.landurbplan.2025.105442

Paper DOI Code

Abstract

This study proposes CoCA, a spatial cooperative simulation framework for modeling and predicting the coupled "land-population-economy" dynamics in urban agglomerations, providing insights into sustainable regional development.
